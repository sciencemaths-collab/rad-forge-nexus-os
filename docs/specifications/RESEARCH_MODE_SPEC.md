# Research Mode Specification

Status: Draft baseline

Research mode compiles a question and protocol into source acquisition, extraction,
claim construction, deterministic computation, synthesis, review, and publication-
ready artifact tasks. It shares the kernel and cannot bypass security, evidence,
approval, or qualification controls.

Each source record includes locator, retrieval time, content digest, license/access
notes, extractor/version, and provenance. Each factual claim links to supporting
source spans or deterministic artifacts and records whether it is direct,
calculated, or inferred. Contradictory evidence is retained and surfaced rather
than overwritten.

Computations declare code/version, parameters, environment, seeds where relevant,
input/output digests, and reproducibility status. Models may propose hypotheses,
search terms, classifications, and prose, but may not fabricate citations or
numerical results. Citation existence, artifact values, and protocol checks are
deterministic gates.

Sensitive human, health, controlled, proprietary, or personally identifiable data
requires explicit data classification, minimized access, provider routing policy,
and approval when leaving the trusted boundary. External submission, messaging,
or publication is always approval-gated.

Acceptance requires reproducible artifacts, claim-to-source coverage, unresolved
conflict reporting, explicit limitations, and an evidence report. Research quality
is not equated with model confidence.
